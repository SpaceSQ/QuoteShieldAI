## Description

Quote Shield AI helps US homeowners check a contractor quote before they sign.

Take a photo, pick one from your library, or upload a PDF. Enter a 5-digit ZIP. Quote Shield AI compares the quote with regional cost benchmarks for five trades: HVAC, electrical panel, smart home, plumbing, and roofing.

You see the contractor total, a rip-off risk score, an estimated savings range, and the titles of red flags right away. Wholesale benchmarks, exact overcharge dollars, and the counter-offer email and text stay locked until you choose a pass.

Project Pro Pass is a one-time $39.99 purchase. Weekly Auditor is $9.99 per week after a 3-day free trial. Cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings.

Quote Shield AI provides statistical cost benchmarks and educational negotiation templates based on regional industry data. It does not constitute legal, engineering, or licensed building inspection advice.
